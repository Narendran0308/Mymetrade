Will I become a professional trader quickly?

Trading skill takes time, practice and emotional control. We will guide you with live sessions, reviews and a clear process, but results depend on your discipline, risk control and consistency.

Can I join the live market sessions?

Yes. You can join daily live sessions, watch the market in real time, ask doubts and understand why a setup is taken or avoided.

Do you provide trading signals?

No blind signals. We focus on teaching simple rule-based models so you understand the reason behind every trade idea.

Which markets can I trade in this mentorship?

We mainly focus on Nifty, Bank Nifty, Gold, Forex and other liquid markets where structure, execution and risk control matter.

Are the educational videos beginner-friendly?

Yes. Lessons are kept simple, structured and beginner-friendly, with examples explained in an easy local style.

Can I cancel anytime?

These are fixed access plans. Please choose your batch carefully before payment and read the refund policy fully.

Refund Policy

Yes 100% Refund. Refunds are reviewed only when the learner has followed the shared process properly.
* Complete the required lessons and follow the explained setup rules for 20 trades.
* A few losing trades are normal because markets are never 100% predictable.
* If you request a review, submit screenshots showing entry, exit, stop-loss and all setup confirmations.
* Refunds cannot be processed if the required rules, risk limits or confirmations were not followed.
* Our goal is to review mistakes honestly and improve the trading process.
